Consumables

Medical consumables sourcing

Bought in volume, used once, and the category where a small saving most often turns into a held consignment.

In short
NJMC sources single use medical consumables from certified manufacturers in China and India, matched to whether CE marking, FDA clearance or SFDA approval is accepted in the destination market. Certification is verified against the manufacturing site named on the certificate before an order is placed.
Consumables carry constant price pressure, which makes the uncertified substitute permanently tempting. The arithmetic rarely holds. A consignment stopped at customs because its certification is not recognised in the destination market costs the purchase price, the freight, the storage, the delay to whatever depended on it, and the cost of starting again.

We source from manufacturers whose certification matches where the goods are actually going, and put that documentation in front of you before an order is committed.

What we source

Single use and disposable supplies for hospital, clinical and laboratory settings.

What we check

Certification that matches the destination. A CE marked device is not automatically acceptable in Saudi Arabia, and an FDA listing is not a CE mark. Our guide to CE, FDA and SFDA sets out what each one actually tells you.

Quality system. ISO 13485 certification of the manufacturing site, with the site named on the certificate matching the site producing your order.

Sterile or non-sterile, and by what method. Where ethylene oxide is used, residual limits and the validation behind them matter.

Biocompatibility. ISO 10993 evidence appropriate to the contact type and duration.

Shelf life and lot traceability. Including whether remaining shelf life on arrival will be acceptable to your end customer.

The question that catches most problems. Do not ask whether a product is certified. Ask for the certificate, then check three things: that it has not expired, that the manufacturer and site named on it match the factory producing your order, and that the product it describes is the product you are buying. Most certification problems are visible on the certificate itself.

Frequently asked questions

Is a CE marked consumable accepted in Saudi Arabia?
Not automatically. The Saudi Food and Drug Authority runs its own process, and approval elsewhere may support an application without replacing it. Establish what the destination market requires before selecting a supplier.
What should I check on a supplier's certificate?
Three things: that it has not expired, that the manufacturer and site named on it match the factory producing your order, and that the product it describes is the product you are buying. Most certification problems are visible on the certificate itself.
Do you verify sterilisation and biocompatibility?
Yes. Where ethylene oxide sterilisation is used, residual limits and validation matter, and ISO 10993 biocompatibility evidence should be appropriate to the contact type and duration.
