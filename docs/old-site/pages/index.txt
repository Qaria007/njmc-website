Delivering Quality Medical Solutions

Markets served across China, Yemen, the Middle East & the UK

 At NJMC Medical Supplies Co., Ltd, we are a team of highly educated medical experts committed to serving hospitals and medical care facilities. We empower their services by supplying required equipment and disposables, and by providing consultancy services that support medical institutions in enhancing their operational efficiency and service quality.

 Based in Nanjing, China, NJMC leads international trading activities for medical supplies sourced from China and India and supplied to overseas markets, with growing capacity in pharmaceutical raw materials, API-related sourcing, documentation coordination, and client communication with global partners.

Our Mission

We are committed to facilitating growth and excellence in healthcare by connecting medical facilities with the supplies and expertise they need to thrive in today's competitive environment.
